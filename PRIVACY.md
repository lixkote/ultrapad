# UltraPad Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 18, 2026

UltraPad is a word processor for Windows made by Lixkote.

## Data collection
UltraPad does not collect, store, or share any personal information. It has no accounts,
analytics, telemetry, or advertising.

## Your documents
Your documents and settings are stored only on your device. UltraPad opens and saves
files only when you choose them. Nothing is uploaded anywhere.

## Internet access
UltraPad only opens web links you click yourself (for example, the source code or
feedback pages). Those websites have their own privacy policies.

## Third parties
UltraPad does not sell or share any data with third parties.

## Changes
If this policy changes, the updated version will be published on this page.

## Contact
If you have any questions, please open an issue at https://github.com/lixkote/UltraPad/issues
